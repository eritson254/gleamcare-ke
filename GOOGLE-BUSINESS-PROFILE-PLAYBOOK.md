## Before Creating the Profile

Complete these items before opening the Google setup screen. This prevents mistakes that can cause edits, re-verification, or suspension.

### Required Access

- Create or use a Google Account controlled by the business owner.
- Recommended account email:
  - Best: a company email if available, for example `hello@gleamcare.ke` or `info@gleamcare.ke`.
  - Acceptable: a dedicated Gmail such as `gleamcarekenya@gmail.com`.
- Do not create the profile under an employee's personal email unless that person is the owner.
- After verification, add trusted managers instead of sharing the owner password.

### Required Real-World Proof

Google requires the business to reflect the real-world business. Prepare:

- Storefront or shop signage showing the business name.
- Photos of the building entrance and nearby landmarks.
- Photos inside the shop showing shelves, stock, counter, packaging, or customer area.
- Proof of management for verification:
  - Unlocking the shop.
  - Opening employee-only area, storage, counter, or point-of-sale area.
  - Showing branded packaging, receipts, business registration, invoices, or utility document with business name. Do not show private identification numbers, bank details, or sensitive customer data.

### Confirm the Real Business Name

Use the name that appears in the real world on signage, receipts, packaging, website, and social accounts.

Recommended:

```text
GleamCare
```

Use this only if signage/branding says it:

```text
GleamCare Kenya
```

Do not use a keyword-stuffed name like:

```text
GleamCare Kenya - Skincare, K-Beauty, Cosmetics & Perfume Shop Nairobi
```

That may look tempting for ranking, but it violates the spirit of Google's naming guidelines and can trigger edits or suspension. We will add keywords through categories, products, services, description, photos, posts, website content, and reviews instead.

## Step 3: Verification Plan

Google chooses verification methods automatically. Common options include video recording, phone/SMS, live video call, or mail/postcard.

### Best Verification Method for GleamCare

Use video verification if offered. It is strong for a storefront business.

Before recording:

- Make sure the shop is open or accessible.
- Clean and arrange the storefront, shelves, counter, and product display.
- Make sure business signage is visible.
- Have keys ready.
- Have proof of management ready.
- Avoid filming customer faces, ID numbers, bank details, private documents, or sensitive customer data.

### Video Verification Shot List

Record one continuous video. Do not edit, pause, or stitch clips.

Recommended sequence:

1. Start outside Mithoo Business Centre.
2. Show Moi Avenue / nearby signs / recognizable landmarks, including opposite the Bazaar if visible.
3. Walk toward the building entrance.
4. Show the shop signage or any permanent sign with `GleamCare`.
5. Enter the shop.
6. Show product shelves or product inventory.
7. Show branded packaging, receipts, shopping bags, or labels.
8. Show proof that you manage the location:
   - unlock the shop, or
   - open the counter/storage area, or
   - access a point-of-sale/cash drawer, or
   - show a non-sensitive invoice/business document with the business name.
9. End after clearly showing the business exists, is at the right place, and you manage it.

Video requirements from Google:

- One continuous recording.
- At least 30 seconds.
- Recorded and uploaded through the Business Profile on a mobile device.
- Shows location, business existence, and proof of management.

### If Video Verification Fails

Do not panic. Read Google's reason carefully and re-record with the missing proof.

Common fixes:

- If Google says business name was not visible, show permanent signage more clearly.
- If Google says location was unclear, start farther outside and show recognizable street/building details.
- If Google says management proof was missing, show keys, staff-only access, stockroom, POS, or non-sensitive business documentation.

## Step 9: Reviews Strategy

Reviews are one of the biggest long-term trust signals. Google says reviews and positive ratings can help local ranking, and customers use them heavily before contacting a business.

### Review Rules

Do:

- Ask real customers for reviews.
- Use Google's review link or QR code.
- Ask after successful delivery or successful in-store purchase.
- Reply to reviews professionally.
- Encourage customers to mention what they bought and their area naturally if they want.

Do not:

- Offer discounts, gifts, refunds, or incentives in exchange for reviews.
- Ask only happy customers while blocking unhappy customers.
- Write fake reviews.
- Ask staff or owners to review the business.
- Pressure customers to change or remove negative reviews.

Google prohibits incentives for reviews. Keep the process clean.

### How to Get the Review Link / QR Code

After verification:

1. Go to the Business Profile.
2. Select `Read reviews`.
3. Select `Get more reviews`.
4. Copy the review link.
5. Download the QR code.

Use the QR code:

- On receipts.
- At the shop counter.
- In packaging inserts.
- At the end of WhatsApp conversations.

## Step 11: Ranking Levers We Can Control

Google says local results are mainly based on relevance, distance, and prominence.

### Relevance

How well the profile matches what someone searched.

Improve relevance by:

- Choosing the best primary category.
- Adding accurate secondary categories.
- Writing a clear business description.
- Adding products in well-named collections.
- Adding services that match customer searches.
- Publishing posts about skincare, K-beauty, body care, fragrance, and Nairobi CBD.
- Linking to matching website pages.
- Keeping FAQs and Q&A accurate.

### Distance

How close the business is to the searcher or searched location.

We cannot manipulate distance. We can:

- Pin the map location accurately.
- Keep the Nairobi CBD address visible.
- Add real service areas for delivery.
- Use the contact page and schema on the website to reinforce the correct location.

### Prominence

How well-known and trusted the business appears.

Improve prominence by:

- Getting real Google reviews consistently.
- Replying to reviews.
- Adding fresh photos and product images.
- Getting local backlinks and mentions.
- Listing the business consistently in directories.
- Building social proof through Instagram/TikTok.

## What Not To Do

Do not:

- Keyword-stuff the business name.
- Create duplicate profiles.
- Use a fake address.
- Hide the address if customers can visit the shop.
- Use categories that do not match the business.
- Buy reviews.
- Offer discounts or gifts for reviews.
- Upload stock photos as if they are the shop.
- Claim medical benefits for beauty products.
- Ignore negative reviews.
- Let hours go stale.
- Change the business name/address/primary category repeatedly after verification.
