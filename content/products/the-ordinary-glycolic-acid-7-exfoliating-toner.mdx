### What it does

The Ordinary Glycolic Acid 7% Exfoliating Toner helps improve the look of texture, dullness, and uneven tone.

### How it fits in your routine

- Use this in your skincare routine during the `Treat` step.
- Best used in the evening.
- Always use sunscreen the next morning.

## How to use

1. Apply after cleansing, ideally in the evening.
2. Sweep over the face and neck with a cotton pad or fingers.
3. Do not rinse.
4. Follow with hydrating and moisturizing steps.
5. Use sunscreen during the day.

Do not use on sensitive, peeling, or compromised skin. Patch test first.
