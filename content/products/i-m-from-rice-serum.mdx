I'm From Rice Serum supports hydrated, brighter-looking skin and works well when used consistently in a daily routine.

1. Apply after cleansing and toner.
2. Smooth an appropriate amount over the face.
3. Pat gently until absorbed.
4. Follow with moisturizer.
5. In the morning, finish with sunscreen.
