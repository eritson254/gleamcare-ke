### What it does

EQQUALBERRY Purple Rice Pore Smoothing Cleansing Oil helps dissolve sunscreen, makeup, excess sebum, and daily buildup.

### How it fits in your routine

- Use this as the first step in your cleansing routine.
- Follow with a water-based cleanser if double cleansing.

## How to use

1. Apply to dry skin.
2. Massage gently to dissolve makeup, sunscreen, and impurities.
3. Add a little water to emulsify.
4. Rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water.
5. Follow with a water-based cleanser if needed.

Patch test first if your skin is sensitive.
