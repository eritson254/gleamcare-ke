Dark spots usually need time, patience, and daily sun protection. A strong serum can help, but the routine around it matters just as much.

## Start with the basics

Before adding several brightening products, make sure the foundation is steady:

- Gentle cleanser
- One tone-focused treatment
- Moisturizer
- Sunscreen every morning

This keeps the routine easier to follow and lowers the chance of irritation.

## Ingredients that can help

### Niacinamide
Niacinamide is popular because it supports a more even-looking tone while also helping the skin barrier. It is often a good choice for beginners.

### Tranexamic acid
Tranexamic acid, often shortened to TXA, is used in many tone-focused skincare products. It fits well in routines for uneven-looking tone and post-blemish marks.

### Alpha-arbutin
Alpha-arbutin is another brightening ingredient often used in dark spot routines. It is usually lightweight and easy to layer.

### Vitamin C
Vitamin C can support a brighter-looking complexion, but some formulas can feel strong. Introduce it slowly if your skin is reactive.

## A simple morning routine

1. Gentle cleanse or rinse.
2. Apply a hydrating toner if you use one.
3. Use a brightening serum if it suits daytime use.
4. Moisturize if needed.
5. Finish with sunscreen.

Sunscreen is the step that protects your progress. Without it, dark spots can look more stubborn because UV exposure keeps triggering uneven tone.

## A simple evening routine

1. Cleanse well, especially if you wore sunscreen.
2. Apply one tone-focused serum.
3. Follow with moisturizer.
4. Keep exfoliation occasional, not daily.

Do not stack every active at once. If you use a brightening serum, avoid adding strong exfoliation on the same night until you know your skin can handle it.

## What progress looks like

In the first few weeks, skin may look more hydrated and balanced. More visible tone changes often take 8 to 12 weeks of consistent use.

## Common mistakes

- Using too many actives at once
- Skipping sunscreen
- Scrubbing dark marks aggressively
- Changing products every week
- Stopping moisturizer because the skin feels oily

## Keep it calm and consistent

Dark spot routines work best when they are steady. Pick one main treatment, protect your skin every morning, and give the routine enough time before deciding whether it works.
